- Learning success pyramid:
    - **Confidence** in your ability to learn, even if you need to go at your own pace
    - **Self-management** - setting up systems and routines beforehand
    - **Learning** - important to set up the first two before you get to this step.
- Look for intrinsic sources of motivation, such as desire for autonomy, mastery, and purpose/impact
- "Inspiration is, invariably, a stronger force than compulsion."
- Focusing on what you will give yourself through learning or your source of inspiration
- Cornell notes method:
    - Two columns - one has notes that you jot down as you study, while the other contains cues - a more organized outline of information
    - Add summary section to the bottom
- 4 steps of learning:
    - Taking notes
    - Editing
    - Analysis
    - Reflection
- Learning needs to be effortful for it to stick
- Elaborative interrogation - constantly asking why and quizzing yourself on the spot
- The Feynman Technique
    - 1. Choose concept 
    - 2. Explain concept in simple terms - this will help reveal your blind spots
    - 3. Identify the gaps in your knowledge
    - 4. Use analogies as needed to explain the concept
- How to read faster
    - Stop subvocalizing - imagine the meaning of the words instead
    - Use index finger
    - Improve peripheral vision with eye exercises
    - Skimming - skip small words, skip through words, skip over filler words
    - Create a game out of improving your reading speed
    - Read in 50-minute increments
- 4 levels of reading
